About the Community

BYTE - Student Body of the EEE Department

BYTE serves as a vital platform for the Union of the Phoenix Branches, uniting students with updates on activities , events and academics related to the EEE Department.

Our Aim: BYTE is the official website representing the Union of the Phoenix Branches at BITS Pilani, Goa Campus dedicated to promoting student participation and sharing information about union activities, events, and initiatives fostering a vibrant campus culture.
